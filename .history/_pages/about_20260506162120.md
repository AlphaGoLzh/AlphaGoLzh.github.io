I am a third-year **Ph.D. student** in Computer Science at [Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://www.ict.ac.cn/), advised by Prof. [Zhaoqi wang](https://www.ict.ac.cn/sourcedb/cn/jssrck/200909/t20090917_2496735.html) and [Yujun Cai](https://vanoracai.github.io/). I am a member of [Laboratory for Advanced Study](https://ict.cas.cn/jssgk/zzjg/kyxt/qzyjsys/js/). My research interest includes computer vision, computer graphics, and machine learning.
